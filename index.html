Who We Are

BUDABELS is a PvP-first guild that fights together, shares loot fairly, and replaces gear after guild content. We welcome new players who show up, listen to callers, and want to grow with a team.

Fight as one

Disciplined Faction Warfare with clear shotcalling and regular practice.

Share the spoils

Transparent loot splits and a regear program for guild content.

Help each other grow

Veterans train recruits on builds, routes and the market.
